# DNS records

Short cheat-sheet used as a demo corpus for the Telegram knowledge agent.

## A and AAAA
A maps a hostname to an IPv4 address. AAAA maps a hostname to IPv6.

## CNAME
CNAME is an alias. The target should be another hostname, not an IP.

## MX, NS, TXT
MX points to mail servers. NS lists authoritative name servers. TXT holds arbitrary text, often SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
